# Supplementary experiment notes — 17 November 2026

## Appendix A1: comparison on the same 100 papers

pipeline_comparison.csv reports proportions (0–1), rather than percentages. Run 1 is the saved pipeline_run2, Run 2 is pipeline_run3. The original 372-finding audit is a separate candidate pool. Both experts jointly accepted 355 findings on all three axes, yielding 144 paper–harm-family pairs across 88 reference-positive papers. Recall measures recovery of these known families, not exhaustive risk recall. Jaccard is micro intersection/union across all 100 papers; paper recall means at least one reference-family match among the 88 reference-positive papers. Additional predictions have not been adjudicated as false positives. Family matching does not establish identical pathways or status agreement.

pipeline_comparison_summary.json retains aggregate reference results, bootstrap intervals, uniform programmatic diagnostics and separate same-round model ratings. Quote anchoring checks case/whitespace-normalized exact substrings in frozen contribution text. Graph anchoring counts graph_exact + graph_anchored. Graph-free configurations have zero graph anchoring by design. Schema conformance partly reflects deterministic filtering.

Compared with single prompt, Run 1 schema conformance improves by 3.00 percentage points (95% CI 1.55–4.70), quotation anchoring by 10.05 (6.08–14.14). Paired family-recall and Jaccard difference intervals include zero for both alternatives. Intervals use 10,000 paired paper bootstrap replicates without multiple-comparison correction. These comparisons support controlled, traceable auditing, not overall detection superiority or necessity of autonomous multi-agent coordination. Historical service snapshots/fallbacks are incomplete, so component-specific causal effects cannot be isolated.

## Appendix A2: evidence thresholds and sensitivity

coverage_thresholds.csv preserves counts and both denominators: all 15,492 records and 15,486 completed audits. Six without text are missing, not negative. Any-triple coverage includes mitigation and NOT_TRIGGERED records. Risk edges use PROMOTES; complete paths require exact normalized connections within the same saved chain (TF→RM→EI or TF→EI). INSTANTIATED is an audit label, not proof of observed harm. coverage_sensitivity.csv removes a fixed ranking of high-frequency risk edges before recomputing paths. The opacity removal is an exploratory lexical proxy, not a formal harm-family ablation.

## Appendix A3: external controls

external_controls.json contains only aggregate results. A: 100 manually screened non-AI EO abstracts, compared with 9,789 main-corpus abstracts weighted to represented journal–year strata. Non-AI instrument/calibration studies differ in application context, which can confound the comparison. C: 25 unrelated mathematics/theoretical-physics abstracts; zero outputs, Wilson 95% CI 0–13.3%, not an estimate of corpus specificity. D: 30 constructed abstract pairs, three runs. The pre-specified T_inst includes mitigation edges; risk-only T_inst_risk and impact-reaching T_inst_ei are post hoc. Majority-vote T_inst rates are 73.3% versus 23.3%, difference +50.0 points (95% CI +30.0–+70.0), one-sided exact McNemar p=0.0001373. Each run is significant (p=0.002–0.011), but individual judgments vary: disagreement is 43%/53% for risk/safe versions under T_inst. The physical-harm pairs do not discriminate in the expected direction under this outcome. Constructed explicit conditions may overstate real-paper discrimination. Controls use the fixed local fast-agent configuration; complete historical identity with every main-corpus request is not established.
