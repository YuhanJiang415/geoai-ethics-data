i

Read each edge, not just the endpoint. A documented technical feature, an instantiated mechanism and an observed harm are different claims. Evidence and status belong to individual assertions.

From input to retained finding: the workflow +
  1. Ontology (S1.1). Each concept gets a role: TechnicalFeature (TF), RiskMechanism (RM), EthicalImpact (EI) or Mitigation (M). Admissible topology: TF —PROMOTES→ RM —PROMOTES→ EI; a mitigation INHIBITS an RM or EI. Only impacts carry a harm family.
  2. Knowledge graph (S1.2). Retrieval returns conditional candidate edges, each with its supporting document. Edges lifted through IS_A inherit that support; weight 1 means one document, not a probability.
  3. Audit and four gates (S1.3). Topology, provenance, quotation and instantiation are applied separately. A failed quotation marks uncertainty and does not delete the triple.
  4. Corpus admission (S1.4). A paper enters the audit when an identifiable geospatial object is paired with a substantive AI component.

Full texts and abstracts are not distributed here. The example quotes short passages from two open-access articles (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) with attribution; all other evidence is summarised. Download the Supplementary Material (PDF) ↗ · S1 walkthrough (Markdown) ↗

What was saved, what was reconstructed

Each saved finding has an exact DOI-plus-suffix ID. Explanatory transitions are reconstructed; complete historical intermediate responses and exact runtime graph identity are unavailable. The graph’s five saved harm families remain separate from derived six-family and eleven-theme analysis layers.
