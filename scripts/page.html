These are selected saved audit judgments. The guide paraphrases the evidence and reconstructs the explanation. A conditional pathway or an INSTANTIATED label does not establish that harm occurred.

What is reported?

The dataset combines street-level imagery with geographic context and automatically derived attributes.

What does the audit retain?

A conditional fusion → disclosure → group privacy pathway. Both saved edges remain POTENTIAL.

What remains unproven?

The contribution does not establish a disclosure event or group privacy harm. The feature alone cannot instantiate the whole chain.

Inspect the evidence checks +

Both edges have the saved provenance label graph_exact and admissible node types. The initial quotation check passes for fusion → disclosure (#2), but fails for disclosure → privacy harm (#3). Neither has a verified instantiation quotation. A genuine quotation can establish a technical configuration while its downstream risk remains potential.

What is reported?

Deep models automatically label visual-contextual attributes across the dataset.

What does the audit retain?

The opacity mechanism (#4) is INSTANTIATED, supported by a separately verified implementation span.

What remains conditional?

The planning-impact edge (#5) is POTENTIAL. An implemented model does not establish harmful planning decisions.

Why can a failed quote check coexist with INSTANTIATED? +

For #4, the initial quotation check fails, while a separate instantiation quotation passes. These checks concern different text fields. The saved provenance is graph_exact; the retrieved opacity edge is a hierarchy-lifted edge. The audit retains the finding with these qualifications.

What is reported?

The study combines imagery sources to improve geographic coverage.

What does the audit retain?

Data integration inhibits algorithmic bias; the mitigation is labelled INSTANTIATED in the saved output.

What does the label mean?

A safeguard is implemented. It does not demonstrate that every source of bias or downstream harm has been removed.

How to read the inhibition connector +

The line ending in a bar points from the mitigation toward the mechanism it inhibits. PROMOTES uses an arrowhead and records a risk-promoting assertion; INHIBITS records a mitigating assertion. Both can be INSTANTIATED. Counts of all instantiated triples therefore combine different kinds of findings and should not be read as a count of operating risk pathways.

Inspect the evidence checks +

The saved provenance is graph_exact. The initial quotation check fails; no instantiation quotation is verified. The original methods describe the explanation module. Both experts accept the saved verdict, while the failed automatic quotation check remains recorded.

i

Read each edge, not just the endpoint. A documented technical feature, an instantiated mechanism and an observed harm are different claims. Evidence and status belong to individual assertions.

From input to retained finding: the workflow +
  1. Read the reported configuration. Identify technical choices in the paper’s contribution.
  2. Retrieve relevant pathways. Use the ontology and graph to connect features, mechanisms, impacts and mitigations.
  3. Check each assertion. Keep topology, provenance, quotation matching and instantiation checks separate.
  4. Retain a qualified finding. Save the relation, condition, evidence fields and status; preserve uncertainty.

Full texts and abstracts are not distributed here. This public example paraphrases selected saved findings rather than exposing source passages. Download the S1 walkthrough ↗

Expert checks and provenance of this example +

Three separate evaluation axes

The two experts pass groundedness, causal validity and verdict correctness for all six findings in paper 026. The model disputes causal validity for #1, bias → marginalization and exclusion; no separate adjudication of this dissent is recorded. A verdict pass means the assigned label is appropriate.

What was saved, what was reconstructed

Findings and ratings are joined by exact DOI-plus-suffix IDs. Explanatory transitions are reconstructed; complete historical intermediate responses and exact runtime graph identity are unavailable. The graph’s five saved harm families remain separate from derived six-family and eleven-theme analysis layers.

Chuan Chen · Yuhan Jiang · Yikun Hao · Puzhen Zhang · Peng Luo · Liqiu Meng

Submitted to the ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing.

Available here

Bibliographic identifiers, descriptive metadata, aggregate audit counts, the causal graph, construction decisions, prompts, ontology, supplementary experiment summaries and a paraphrased workflow example. Six records with empty abstracts are flagged as unaudited.

Publication boundaries

Full texts, abstracts and verbatim source passages are excluded. Corpus findings are aggregated; the selected Supplementary S1 walkthrough is an explicit paper-level example. Full per-paper audit outputs remain excluded; source-document attribution is retained.
